# THE LONG GRACE — PLATES OF THE TABLE: Header Art Bible — Writers' Brief

We are making a set of **cinematic header plates** for *The Long Grace* (formerly "the Gorging World"): wide 3:1 horizontal images, all with the **exact same frame** (a thin gold double-line border with diamond corners, the land's sigil in a notch at top centre, the title in widely-spaced light sans capitals, a subtitle in italic serif beneath). Each plate is a dark painterly illustration in the style of these references (described for you):

- **Reference 1:** a pale, cold, near-monochrome portrait (blue-grey, bone-white) of a face with a stained bandage over the eyes and the cheeks torn open into a ring of too many long yellow teeth, raw red-brown flesh at the tear; neck seamed with a dark split and scaly ridges. Oil-paint brushwork, soft rim light, black background. Intimate body horror.
- **Reference 2:** a hooded, ragged figure in heavy dark cloth, face hidden behind a leather mask with round amber glass eyes and a ribbed hose; high-contrast silhouette bleeding into white paper and ink splatter. Desaturated with one warm accent.
- **Reference 3:** a shaven-headed woman, chalk-white skin with a rash of rust-red mottling on the scalp, a third eye opened in the forehead, a long crude stitched seam across one eye and cheek; bone, tooth and shell ornaments hanging from the ears. Soft painterly light, violet-black background. Ritual, beauty and wrongness.
- **Reference 4:** a black-and-white wide strip of crooked wooden grave crosses leaning in long grass against a pale sky: ink-wash silhouettes, dry-brush texture, scratchy.
- **Reference 5:** a near-black vertical image with a ghostly half-face in a gas-mask emerging from shadow at the right, wet highlights, and a small epigraph in spaced serif capitals at lower left (WE DO NOT KNOW, IF WE ARE STILL ALIVE). Huge negative space.

**Shared style:** dark, painterly, gritty brushwork and ink texture; heavy black negative space; desaturated near-monochrome with ONE accent colour per land; cinematic wide composition; body horror and decay rendered intimately; no bright colours; no cartoon; no clean digital gloss.

Land accent colours: Sallowreach — bruise-green and fly-black on tea-stain sepia; Fatlands — raw meat red and tallow yellow; Brinehollow — drowned teal and salt-white; the Vigil — lamp amber and smoke; Cradlewrack — wet iron red and clay; Oathen — canyon ochre and dried-blood brown; the Fast — warm candle gold (the only warm, kind light, which is the horror); the Eighth/world — bone-white on pure black.

**Read for canon** (grep/skim, do not read everything): `/home/user/newgame/source/gorging_bible.txt`, the land chapters in `/home/user/newgame/book3/` (e.g. `20_sallowreach.md`), and the companion `/home/user/newgame/book2/`. Use real names, places and details. Do not reveal the Eighth (the Guest); hint only.

## Content

The headers are horror. Describe visceral, sensory detail (rot, flesh, bone, transformation, desecration, the aftermath of ritual killing, the grief left by suicide and madness — aftermath only, never method). Sexual taboo and necrophilia: never depicted, at most implied by an object or absence. No sexualised depiction of anyone; no gore on children. The **Painter's Prompt** must be usable by an artist or image model: evocative and specific, with the gore described as painterly rather than clinical, and a **negative prompt** listing what to avoid (bright colours, cartoon, text artefacts, modern objects, etc.).
