# Plates of the Table: The Header Art Bible

> We do not paint the god. We paint the plate it was served on, and let you imagine the rest.
>> — note pinned above a Cutwright illuminator's desk, Quire

[plate: world]

This book defines the **header plates** of *The Long Grace*: wide 3:1 title images for the setting's gods and places, all set in one shared frame so the books, handouts, screens and online pages read as a single object.

Each entry in the following chapters gives you two things:

- **The plate as it stands.** A finished title plate: the shared gold frame, the land's sigil, and the title lettering, over an ink-and-fog scene drawn in code. These are usable now for chapter headers, slides, a virtual table or printed handouts.
- **The brief for the painting it is waiting for.** A cinematic art brief for a painter or an image model: the shot, the light, the textures, the story in the frame, the hidden details a sharp-eyed player could find, and a ready-to-use prompt with a negative prompt. The style follows the references the plates were designed against: dark painterly brushwork, heavy black space, near-monochrome with one accent colour per land, and body horror rendered close and intimate.

When a painting is commissioned, set it under the frame overlay supplied with the plates (`_frame_overlay_blank.png`), keep the lower centre band dark and calm for the title, and the new plate will match every other plate in the set.

## The Frame

Every plate uses exactly the same frame and title treatment. Do not vary it.

| Element | Specification |
| Canvas | 2400 × 800 px (3:1); safe area inset 60 px |
| Outer rule | 2.2 px gold #b79c63, inset 26 px |
| Inner rule | 1.2 px dim gold #7d6a45, inset 44 px, diamond at each inner corner, short gold bars beside each corner |
| Sigil notch | 140 px gap at top centre; land sigil in light gold #d8bf86, line-drawn |
| Kicker | THE LONG GRACE · LAND, Josefin Sans 400, 17 px, tracking 9 |
| Title | Josefin Sans Light 300, 70 px, all capitals, tracking 22, light gold, centred at y 652 |
| Rule | dim gold line either side of centre with three small diamonds, y 684 |
| Subtitle | Cormorant Garamond italic, 34 px, parchment #cfc4ad, y 726 |
| Title band | soft black ellipse behind the lettering; keep the painting quiet here |

[box: How to Use the Briefs]
- **For artists:** read *The Shot*, *Light and Colour* and *Texture and Flesh* first; *The Story in the Frame* tells you what the moment means; *Hidden Details* are the things players will look for.
- **For image models:** use the *Painter's Prompt* and *Negative* as written, at a 3:1 aspect ratio, then place the result under the blank frame overlay.
- **For the Carver:** read *The Story in the Frame* aloud when the Guests first arrive somewhere. Every brief is also a location description.
[/box]
