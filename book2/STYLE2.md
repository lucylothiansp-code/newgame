# THE LONG GRACE: SEVEN TABLES — Writers' Brief for the Second Book

This is the second book for the horror tabletop RPG *The Long Grace*. The first book (the core book) is in `/home/user/newgame/book/` — its writers' bible `/home/user/newgame/book/STYLE.md` still applies in full (READ IT FIRST: tone, content lines, originality rule, the RULES numbers, markup format, canon). The setting source is `/home/user/newgame/source/gorging_bible.txt`.

The second book is titled **THE LONG GRACE — SEVEN TABLES: Lands, Lives & Leavings**. It has two halves:
1. **The Seven Tables** — an even deeper focus on each land and, above all, on **every unique character of each land in great depth**: who they are, their stats, what they carry, how they function inside their land, their quotes, small blurbs, relationships, secrets, and how to use them in play.
2. **Leavings** — an "artifacts" catalogue: relics harvested from gods, godsign-touched people, places and horrors, with lore, rules for knowing of them, carving (harvesting) them, how fast they spoil, Taint, effects and worth. (Format inspired by big "artifact" supplements, but all concepts original.)

## Consistency (very important)

The core book already describes every land and gave short dossiers and stat blocks for its characters. **Read the core-book chapter for your land in full before writing** (e.g. `/home/user/newgame/book/20_sallowreach.md`). Your work must agree with it: same facts, ages, secrets, stat numbers (you may refine stats slightly but don't contradict), canon choices. Go much deeper than it did; do not simply repeat it. Also skim `/home/user/newgame/book/50_bazaar.md` (items, prices, currency, Taint rules) and `/home/user/newgame/book/30_creation.md` + `40_rules.md` (rules) so that what characters carry uses real items and real rules.

## Content

Same as the core book: graphic, visceral, sensory horror is wanted (body horror, gore, murder, ritual killing, kin-slaying, cannibalism, desecration, blasphemy, oaths on the dead, contagious madness, suicide as tragedy and never instruction, psychological horror, possession, starvation, no rescue). Sexual taboo / incestuous bloodlines / necrophilia stay implied and adults-only. Never sexual content involving minors; do not linger on graphic gore inflicted on children. No Degenesis or other published-game concepts or terms (avoid even words like "primer", "sepsis", "burn", "spore", "clan", "potential", "ego", "trigger", "legends", "deterioration", "harvest" as rules terms).

## Markup

Exactly the core book's markup (see STYLE.md): one paragraph per line; `#`, `##`, `###`, `####` headings; `>` epigraph lines and `>> — attribution`; `- ` bullets; `**bold**`, `*italic*`; `[box: Title]...[/box]`; `[fiction]...[/fiction]`; `[stat: Name — Title] ... [/stat]` with `Label: value` lines; `[quick] ...`; `| table | rows |` (header first, max 5 columns, no tables inside boxes); `[pagebreak]`. Additionally available in this book:
- `[bigquote] Text of a quote | — Attribution` — a large decorative full-width quotation (use one or two per chapter for drama).
- `[sigil: name]` — places the decorative sigil of a land (`sallowreach`, `fatlands`, `brinehollow`, `vigil`, `cradlewrack`, `oathen`, `fast`, `rim`, `eighth`). Put one under major section openings in the Leavings chapters.
Do not invent any other tags.

## CHARACTER DOSSIER TEMPLATE (the heart of this book)

Every character gets this full treatment (aim 900–1,300 words for major characters; never under 700):

```
### Full Name — Epithet
> "A characteristic quote in their own voice."
>> — Name, to whom / when
[stat: Full Name — Epithet]
Land & Cut: e.g. Sallowreach · Cut 5 (Hand-line) · Regrowth 6 (the Appetite) · Hunger 4
Age & Station: ...
Calling & Standing: (map to a core-book Calling and Standing 1–5, or "none")
Attributes: Hand 2 · Gut 3 · Lung 1 · Eye 4 · Tongue 5 · Caul 5
Skills: Clause 5, Persuade 4, Lore 4, ...
Flesh 14 · Guard 11 · Armor 2 · Fray 7
Attacks: ...
Gifts: (godsign powers, with mechanics)
Wants: (with triggers)
Knacks: two or three from the core book list
Derangements: (if any)
Carries: (short list; detailed below)
Dread: N (and why)
[/stat]
#### Who They Are
(appearance in sensory detail: smell, sound of voice, texture of skin, how they move; mannerisms; how others perceive them)
#### Their Story
(life history; how they became what they are; key incidents with dates in A.G.)
#### Their Place in the Land
(how they function inside the land's institutions, economy, law and daily life: who depends on them, who they depend on, their routine, their power and its limits)
#### What They Carry
- **Item** — what it is, where it came from, what it means to them, any rules (cost/Taint if relevant).
(5–10 items, mixing the mundane, the personal and the terrible)
#### Wants, Fears and Secrets
(include at least one secret the GM can reveal, and one the character doesn't know)
#### Ties
- **Other character** — the relationship in one or two sentences. (4–8 ties, across their land and beyond)
#### In Play
(how PCs meet them; what they want from PCs; three scene seeds or hooks; how they react to threat, kindness, and the god; what happens to them if nobody intervenes)
[box: Said of Them]
"Short quote about them by someone else." — Speaker
(three quotes from different people, including at least one commoner)
[/box]
```

Use the land's own idiom, proverbs and small blurbs throughout. Minor characters may be shorter (500–700 words) but still use all headings.

## LAND DEEP-DIVE TEMPLATE (for the writers assigned the "a" half of a land)

Before the dossiers, write a land deep-dive (8,000–10,000 words) that goes further than the core book. Do not repeat its history and factions; instead write about **how the land works at ground level**:
- `# [Land]: [poetic subtitle]` + epigraph + an atmospheric `[fiction]` opening (a day in the life).
- **Districts and Settlements** — a detailed gazetteer of the capital (6–10 districts/quarters) and 6–8 other towns/villages, each with who lives there, what it smells and sounds like, what it trades, a local custom, a local danger, and one notable NPC (new minor NPCs with `[quick]` lines).
- **Roads and Ways** — how people travel inside the land; a table of routes with days and dangers.
- **Bread and Coin** — the land's economy: trades, wages (a table of what common jobs pay per day in lacks), what things cost locally, taxes, who's rich.
- **Law and the Knife** — crimes and punishments table, how a trial works, who enforces.
- **The Faithful** — local religion: the three doctrines locally, shrines, rites, holy days, a prayer or hymn text.
- **Voices of the Land** — 12–20 short quotes/blurbs from ordinary people (a gravedigger, a child, a whore, a soldier, a mother...) each one or two lines, as a bulleted list or in boxes.
- **How Power Moves** — a section diagramming who controls whom: a table of the land's main power-holders, what they control, what they fear, who they owe.
- Then begin the dossiers under `## The People of [Land]` (first half of the cast).

The "b" half writer continues the cast under `## The People of [Land], Continued` and ends the land with:
- `## New Faces of [Land]` — dossiers for every NEW character the core-book chapter added for this land, plus 2 more of your own.
- `## Webs of [Land]` — a relationship section: who loves, hates, owes and hunts whom, as a table (Character | Wants from | Fears | Owes) plus a short narrative of the three most explosive relationships.
- `## Using [Land]'s People` — d20 table "Who's at the Door?" picking a character plus a reason.

## LEAVINGS (ARTIFACT) ENTRY TEMPLATE

Leavings are grouped by land, then by source (e.g. "From the Kept", "From the Hand-lines", "From the Hushes"). Each source group starts with `## Source Group Name`, `[sigil: land]`, and an intro paragraph. Each artifact:

```
### Artifact Name
(2–4 paragraphs of lore: what it is, where it comes from, a legend/story of someone who used it, the sensory horror of it)
**WHISPERED:** Difficulty to have heard of it — Eye + Lore vs Hard (14), etc.
**SPOILAGE:** how long it lasts once taken, and how to preserve it (salt, brine, wax, lead, cold, keeping it on a living body...)
**CARVING:** how to take it, from what or whom, with what tools, the roll (Attribute + Skill vs Difficulty), what a Lack does, and the Dread check involved
**TAINT:** rating 0–5 and type (Hunger / Regrowth / Dread), per the Rim Market chapter's rules
**EFFECT:** the exact rules effect, duration, uses
**WORTH:** price in lacks (or "never sold"), availability (Common/Uncommon/Scarce/Rare/Unique), where it is traded, legality
```
Some entries can carry a `[fiction]` snippet (a letter, a ledger, a confession) or a small d6 table of random results ("When you swallow it, roll 1d6: ...").
