## Content

Same as STYLE3: visceral, on-page sensory horror (body horror and gore; murder, ritual killing, kin-slaying, torture; cannibalism, grave desecration, blasphemy, oaths on the dead, human sacrifice, betrayal of kin; suicide, self-harm and contagious madness only as TRAGEDY, never methods; psychosis, delusions, lost time, fractured identity, obsession, grief, sadism, gaslighting, cult control; possession, damnation, starvation, decay, hungry gods, no rescue; at least one worthless sacrifice somewhere in your section). Sexual taboo, incestuous bloodlines, necrophilia: implied only, adults only, never explicit. Nothing sexual involving minors; no lingering graphic gore on children. Real-world-usable content (real recipes, real game rules) must be safe and ordinary: the real recipes are normal food.

Be creative, specific, funny-grim, and *useful at the table*: every item should carry a hook, a rule, or a scene.

## Markup

Core markup (STYLE.md) + `[bigquote] text | — attribution`, `[sigil: land]` (sallowreach, fatlands, brinehollow, vigil, cradlewrack, oathen, fast, rim, eighth), `[pull] text | — attribution`, plus two new tags for this book:
- `[card: Title | Deck name | Card text]` — one printable card (one line). Consecutive card lines are laid out as a grid of cards, 3 per row. Keep card text under ~55 words. Use `**bold**` inside for a rules effect.
- `[entry: Word | part of speech or tag | Definition text]` — one dictionary entry (one line). Definitions may include *italic* example usage and a land tag in the second field (e.g. `n., Sallowreach`).

One `#` chapter title per file (you may have several `#` chapters in one file if assigned). One paragraph per line. No tables in boxes. Max 5 table columns. No `---`.
